Why The Gifted Academy

Enrolling a child usually means three offices and a folder.

One office takes the application, another checks the documents, a third schedules the assessment, and someone re-types it all by hand. The Gifted Academy does the whole loop in one place — apply online, upload the requirements, follow each step as it happens, and get your result when it's done. Less running between offices, fewer forms filled out twice, nothing lost along the way.

What it looks like in use

Every enrollment, accounted for.

The Gifted Academy records each step of enrollment as it happens — an application received, documents verified, an assessment scheduled, the final confirmation — so nothing about enrollment is a mystery.
